About me

Background

Hi, it’s Amanda. You can call me Mandy.

Big fan of the internet, Othello, and humans. I also skate.

Currently I’m a psychology undergrad at SDSU. I’m a research volunteer across a few labs— JSBCAI, the Kappenman lab, and the Center for Tobacco and the Environment; looking at how public health, artificial intelligence, and neuroimaging play a role in clinical psychology and mental health.

I also enjoy social science history and theory, abstract strategy board games (Othello/Reversi), LLMs, statistics, personality models, psychometrics, neuroimaging, and humans in general. My influences include my mentors; Dr. Georg Matt, Dr. Lydia Greiner, and Dr. Emily Kappenman, along with other various cyber/internet science folk; Mary Aiken, Hank Green, and Alok Kanojia (or, HealthyGamerGG).

You can also call me Mandy. Education is for everyone.

Research

Neural Correlates of Cognitive Control in Healthy Adults (ongoing)

Cognitive Affective Psychopathology & Psychophysiology Lab, Kappenman Laboratory, San Diego State University

Mentor: Dr. Emily Kappenman, PhD

I prepared and documented raw EEG datasets for preprocessing and analysis, collected and managed EEG datasets from healthy adult participants during a cognitive control task, and monitored task performance while ensuring high-quality EEG signal acquisition during experimental sessions.

09/2025 — 05/2026Kearny Mesa

Tobacco Smoke Remediation Practices in San Diego County (ongoing)

Policy Research Center for Tobacco and the Environment, San Diego State University

Mentors: Dr. Georg Matt, PhD, Dr. Lydia Greiner, DrPH — with Savannah Yurcek, Amelia Allison, and Samuel Padilla

I developed a database of 100 remediation and cleaning businesses and certified professionals using IICRC records, business directories, and public databases, and prepared the background and methods sections of a mixed-methods study investigating residential tobacco smoke remediation practices and thirdhand smoke contamination. I also contributed to recruitment protocol design and implemented purposive and snowball sampling strategies to recruit professionals with tobacco smoke remediation experience.

09/2025 — 05/2026Kearny Mesa

Smoking and Vaping Policies in Newly Constructed, State-Funded Multiunit Housing in California

Policy Research Center for Tobacco and the Environment, San Diego State University

Mentors: Dr. Georg Matt, PhD, Dr. Lydia Greiner, DrPH — with Gunnar Wooldridge

I integrated administrative and survey data from 281 newly constructed state-subsidized housing developments to evaluate the implementation of smoking and vaping policies, conducted structured qualitative coding of interview and survey data to classify lease policies and enforcement procedures into thematic categories, and synthesized survey responses from 57 California affordable housing properties to identify patterns in lease policy inclusion and enforcement.

Awarded Top Poster Presentation in the College of Sciences at the 2026 SDSU Student Symposium (S3), and also presented at the Third Annual Research Symposium on Tobacco and the Environment.
