About me

Background

Hi, it’s Amanda. You can call me Mandy.

Big fan of the internet, Othello, and humans. I also skate.

Currently I’m a psychology undergrad at SDSU. I’m a research volunteer across a few labs— JSBCAI, the Kappenman lab, and the Center for Tobacco and the Environment; looking at how public health, artificial intelligence, and neuroimaging play a role in clinical psychology and mental health.

I also enjoy social science history and theory, abstract strategy board games (Othello/Reversi), LLMs, statistics, personality models, psychometrics, neuroimaging, and humans in general. My influences include my mentors; Dr. Georg Matt, Dr. Lydia Greiner, and Dr. Emily Kappenman, along with other various cyber/internet science folk; Mary Aiken, Hank Green, and Alok Kanojia (or, HealthyGamerGG).

You can also call me Mandy. Education is for everyone.

Toolkit: R · HTML · JavaScript · SPSS
