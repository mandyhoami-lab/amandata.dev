How to play

  • Black moves first. Tap a highlighted square to place your disc.
  • Your move must trap at least one opponent disc between your new disc and one of yours — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  • Every trapped disc flips to your color.
  • No legal move? You pass automatically.
  • The game ends when neither side can move. Most discs wins.
  • The robot is still learning too — some games it goofs off, some games it locks in.

Tips

  1. Corners are forever. A corner disc can never be flipped. Take one whenever you legally can.
  2. Build on your corners. Discs sheltered by a corner often become unflippable “stable discs.”
  3. Never gift a corner. Your opponent can only take one if you play next to it first.
  4. Beware the X-square. The diagonal neighbor of a corner (b2, g2, b7, g7) — playing there early usually surrenders the corner.
  5. Respect the C-square. The edge square beside a corner is dangerous while the square between it and the corner sits empty. Safe once you hold the edge yourself.
  6. Stay quiet early. Discs touching empty squares (“frontier discs”) hand your opponent moves. Flip less now, choose more later.

Tips adapted from Othello: A Minute to Learn… A Lifetime to Master by Brian Rose. Read the book (free PDF)
