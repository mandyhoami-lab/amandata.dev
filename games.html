Pick a color, then outflank the AI. Black moves first. Add a username to keep an all-time scoreboard on this device.

How to play

  • Black moves first. Tap a highlighted square to place your disc.
  • Your move must trap at least one opponent disc between your new disc and one of yours — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
  • Every trapped disc flips to your color.
  • No legal move? You pass automatically.
  • The game ends when neither side can move. Most discs wins.
